## 1. Target role: Technical Business Analyst

A Technical Business Analyst connects business needs with feasible technology outcomes. Typical work includes stakeholder workshops, requirements elicitation, current/future-state process modelling, user stories and acceptance criteria, traceability, solution validation and testing support.

This role fits my experience documenting BPMN processes, gathering requirements, improving workflows and collaborating with cross-functional technical teams. I use **Technical Business Analyst**, rather than **Senior Business Analyst**, because the latter commonly asks for broader multi-year leadership and complex-program experience.
