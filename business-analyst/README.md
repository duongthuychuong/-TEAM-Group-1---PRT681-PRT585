# Maris Nguyen — Business Analyst Role Portfolio

This folder contains only individual Business Analyst evidence organised by the lecturer's Week 1–4 topics. The case-study product is **CareerTrack NT**, a job-application tracker used for BA research, requirements, process modelling, traceability and evaluation.

CareerTrack NT is an individual draft unless the BA group confirms it as the shared product. Meeting records, presentations, team decisions, course completion and timesheet hours must be added only after they occur.

## Folder guide

- `Week 1`: career research, learning plan, product discovery, processes, personas, user stories and initial backlog.
- `Week 2`: detailed requirements, BABOK notes, BA tools, tailored resume content and interview preparation.
- `Week 3`: traceability, UAT, usability review and salary/benefits comparison template.
- `Week 4`: security/identity requirements, final handover and reflection.
- `WEEKLY_EVIDENCE_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`: blank BA-only individual/group evidence template.

The original `Week 1/PRT681.docx` is preserved as an earlier draft. New Markdown files correct its two main gaps: target roles are not labelled senior, and the learning plan covers three weeks rather than ten.
