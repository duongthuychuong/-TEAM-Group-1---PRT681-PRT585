## Validation habit
Before trusting a dashboard:
1. Check row count.
2. Check total sales in the source.
3. Compare Power BI totals against Excel/SQL.
4. Test filters and slicers.
5. Look for blanks or unexpected categories.

## Main lesson
A dashboard is only useful when the underlying data is clean and the totals can be validated.
