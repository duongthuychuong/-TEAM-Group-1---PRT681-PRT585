Student Career Preparation Tasks 

 
High-Level Summary 
The objective is to build job readiness by researching target roles, learning the required skills, creating role-specific resumes, and preparing for interviews. 

Part A: Career Research & Learning Plan (Week 1) 
Research two target roles, identify required skills and technologies, find learning resources, and create a structured learning plan.

Please see a sample of data analyst role reasearch and 4 week plan.. Its pretty rough. Sample - Data Analyst  

Part B: Resume Preparation (Week 2) 
Create two tailored resumes that showcase your skills, training, projects, and career objectives for each target role. 

Part C: Interview Preparation (Week 2) 
Prepare behavioural and technical interview questions and answers based on your resume, projects, and target roles. 


Key Principle 
If it is on your resume, you must be able to confidently explain what it is, why it is used, how you used it, and the value it provides. 

 

Success Formula 
Research → Learn → Practice (Projects/Tutorials) → Update Resume → Prepare Interview Answers → Practice → Confidence ✅ 

 

Part A: Career Research & Learning Plan Week1 

1. Choose Two Target Roles 

Select two job roles you want to pursue. 

2. Research the Market 

Use: 

SEEK 

LinkedIn Jobs 

Indeed 

Copilot/AI 

Industry professionals and networking contacts 

Identify: 

Key responsibilities 

Required skills 

Tools and technologies 

Certifications commonly requested 

3. Create a Skills & Resources Table 

For each role, prepare a table showing: 

Tool/Technology 

Purpose 

Learning Resource 

SQL 

Data querying 

LinkedIn Learning, YouTube 

Power BI 

Reporting 

Microsoft Learn, YouTube 

4. Build a 3-Week Learning Plan 

Create a simple study plan covering all identified tools. 

Focus: Breadth over depth. Gain a basic understanding of each tool. 

5. Create Tool Notes 

For every tool learned: 

What it is 

Why it is used 

Common use cases 

A simple example 

         (3–4 lines per tool) 

 

 

Part B: Resume Preparation (Next Week - Week2) 

Prepare Two Role-Specific Resumes 

Create two separate resumes, one for each target role. 

Resume Requirements 

1. Skills Section 

Combine and add all tools and technologies identified from your research for both roles. 

Ensure the skills match industry job advertisements. 

2. Certifications / Training Section 

Add all completed courses. 

Include the titles of all or any :  

LinkedIn Learning courses 

Microsoft Learn modules 

YouTube tutorials 

Other online training completed 

Even if a course is not formally certified, list it under Training, Professional Development, or Certifications. 

3. Projects / Experience Section 

Add every practical activity completed during learning. 

Include:  

Hello World projects 

Practice exercises 

Tutorials followed 

Mini assignments 

Portfolio projects 

For each project, include: 

What was built 

Tools used 

Key learning outcomes 

4. Objective Statement 

Clearly state: 

The target role 

Relevant training completed 

 
 

Part C: Interview Preparation (Next Week - Week2) 

C1: Behavioural Questions 

Create a list of the 20 most common behavioural interview questions. 

Examples: 

Tell me about yourself. 

How do you handle stress? 

Describe a challenging situation. 

How do you manage multiple priorities? 

Tell us about a conflict within a team. 

Prepare answers using CAR (Context, Action, Result) or STAR format. 

 
 

C2: Technical Questions 

Create a list of 20 technical questions for each target role. 

Include questions about: 

Tools on your resume 

Projects completed 

Certifications completed 

Technologies learned 

Anything on your resume can be asked in an interview. 

 
 

Key Principle 

For every tool, course, or project on your resume, be able to explain: 

What it is 

Why it is used 

How you used it 

What value it provides
